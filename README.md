# English

## What is this

"AI slop" refers to the formulaic, instantly recognizable output of AI-generated content. It's everywhere now: reports, blog posts, comment sections, marketing copy.

This repo collects typical AI catchphrases and garbage AI-written articles. Partly for fun, partly as a reference: what kind of writing makes people think "an AI wrote this" at first glance.

## Typical patterns

**Catchphrases**

- "In conclusion", "It's worth noting that", "All things considered"
- "Let's dive into...", "Firstly... Secondly... Lastly..."
- "In today's fast-paced world", "It's no secret that"
- Forced uplifting endings: "Let's embrace the bright future that AI brings!"

**Buzzwords**

- empower, leverage, unlock, game-changer, deep dive

**Article traits**

- Bullet-point avalanches with a bold mini-heading for every paragraph; neat structure, zero information
- Always exactly three arguments, each followed by a token example
- Nothing wrong with any single sentence, nothing memorable after reading the whole thing

The list will keep growing. Contributions welcome.
